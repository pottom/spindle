No free icon set carries percussion — not Tabler, not Phosphor, not Font
Awesome — and a row of instruments with no kick at one end and no cymbal at the
other is missing the two things it was for. These three are drawn in the same
hand as the rest: 2 units of stroke on a 24 unit grid, round caps and joins.
