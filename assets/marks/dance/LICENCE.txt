Drawn for spindle, and ours to use.

Seven dancers by one hand, which is the point of them: a row assembled out of an
icon library is seven strangers standing next to each other, and the eye reads
the differences in build instead of the differences in pose. These share a build
and a pen, so the pose is all there is left to read.

They arrive as pictures rather than as paths, so the pen is measured off the
source rather than set per size. The source row is kept beside them in source.png
— everything here is cut from it.
